# Catgirls from Space

A full conversion mod for [Cultist Simulator](https://store.steampowered.com/app/718670/Cultist_Simulator/) by Weather Factory.

> *You discover the secret world of alien catgirls fighting alien serpents. What side will you take?*

This mod serves as a prototype for the standalone *Catgirls from Space* game. The mod is released under the MIT license; the final standalone game will use a different license.

## Overview

**Catgirls from Space** replaces the Lovecraftian occultism of Cultist Simulator with a narrative about uncovering alien factions hidden in modern society. You play as an ordinary person who stumbles upon the existence of immortal catgirls locked in conflict with corrupting serpentine forces aligned with "the Hand" -- a malevolent embodiment of control and unchecked capitalism. Your choices determine which faction you support, what you become, and how the story ends.

This is a **full conversion** -- new legacies, lore, characters, mechanics, books, expeditions, endings, and more. Very little of the original game's content remains.

## Features

### 7 Playable Legacies

| Legacy | Description |
|---|---|
| **Conspiracy Theorist** | A retail worker who stumbles onto the truth. The default starting experience. |
| **'Self Made' Billionaire** | Wealthy and well-connected, but danger follows money. |
| **The Physician** | A medical professional with a stable income -- recommended for players who find survival difficult. |
| **The Detective** | A police inspector investigating strange crimes. Story-rich and dangerous. |
| **The Dancer** | A young performer at the Gaiety Theatre. Progression-focused for early game. |
| **The Exile** | A refugee who learned a horrible truth and is on the run. Starts mid-game with Exile DLC content. |
| **Cryonicist** | Continue a frozen leader's legacy and work to revive them. |

Additional post-game legacies unlock after completing certain endings.

### 12 Reimagined Principles

The mod replaces Cultist Simulator's original aspects with its own thematic system:

- **Hand** -- Control, materialism, the invisible hand of markets
- **Heart (The Pride)** -- Vitality, community, human connection
- **Winter** -- Calm, introspection, mental hibernation, cryonics
- **Secret Histories** -- Hidden truths, reframing perspectives, enlensing
- **Lantern** -- Enlightenment, knowledge, illumination
- **Grail** -- Desire, sensation, physical experience
- **Edge** -- Overcoming challenges, flow state, resilience
- **Knock** -- Transitions, breaking barriers, liberation
- **Forge** -- Transformation, self-reinvention
- **Moth** -- Secrets, fluid identity, larping
- **Dance** -- Heart and Moth combined; older than language, younger than life
- **Imbalance** -- Alien corruption, the wrong kind of change

### Ideological Paths (Veneration)

Your choices align you with one of several factions:

- **Catgirl** -- Fight alongside the immortal catgirls against the Hand
- **Hand** -- Serve the invisible hand and manipulate markets and people
- **Secret Histories** -- Seek hidden truths through enlensing and perspective shifts
- **Imbalance** -- Embrace alien corruption and the serpent overlords

### Major Game Systems

- **Cryonics** -- Freeze and revive followers. Build and maintain cryonics facilities. A path to preservation and commitment.
- **Romance & Polycule** -- Build multi-partner relationships with named characters. Take followers on dates to specific locations.
- **Capers & Expeditions** -- Plan and execute heists requiring specific skills, followers, and resources. Explore vaults with aspect-based obstacle resolution.
- **Influencer / YouTube** -- Build an online channel to spread ideology and gain followers through the internet.
- **Enlensing** -- Shift perspectives and reframe experiences through different lenses. Invert and transform lore types.
- **Dreaming & the Mansus** -- Explore dream realms through the Stag Door. Multiple ascension paths requiring sacrifice and commitment.
- **Book & Study System** -- Translate texts in ancient languages (Latin, Greek, Fucine, Phrygian, Aramaic, and more). Research projects with branching challenge types.
- **Detective Investigations** -- Track suspects, gather evidence, and resolve cases (Detective legacy).
- **Cult & Society Management** -- Build organizations, manage reputation, and trade with other factions.

### 20+ Named Characters

A full cast of recruitable, romanceable, and story-relevant characters including Lilith, Evelyn, Ethan, Arthur, Beatrice, Clovette, Violet, and many more -- each with their own aspects and narrative roles.

### Multiple Endings

Over a dozen endings across several victory paths:

- **Forge Ascension** -- Pass through the Savage Door and become a Name of the Forge
- **Grail Ascension** -- Dwell in the Grail's chambers eternally
- **Lantern Ascension** -- Become the light in the Watchman's hand
- **Heart Victory** -- Join the Thunderskin's eternal chorus
- **Moth Victory** -- Transform into something with wings and scales
- **Catgirl Revolution** -- Destroy the Hand and end capitalism
- **Capitalist Ascension** -- Become an enforcer of the Hand
- **Balance / Meniscate** -- Find equilibrium in the shadow-Mansus
- ...and more, including variant endings with a risen beloved

Failure endings include dissolution (madness), death (starvation), and despair.

## Installation

1. Subscribe to the mod on the [Steam Workshop](https://store.steampowered.com/app/718670/Cultist_Simulator/), **or** download/clone this repository.
2. If installing manually, place the mod folder in your Cultist Simulator mods directory:
   - **Windows:** `%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Weather Factory\Cultist Simulator\mods\`
   - **macOS:** `~/Library/Application Support/Weather Factory/Cultist Simulator/mods/`
   - **Linux:** `~/.config/unity3d/Weather Factory/Cultist Simulator/mods/`
3. Launch Cultist Simulator. The mod should appear in the mods menu.
4. Enable the mod and start a new game.

> **Note:** This is a full conversion mod. It is not designed to be played alongside other content mods.
